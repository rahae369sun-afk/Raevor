Read About Me






I’m Zahra, although most people know me as Raha!



An aspiring Indie Game Developer with a background in Computer Science and a passion for creating games, characters, and immersive worlds.

My journey into game development began during high school ; where I created my first game and won 1st Place at the 43rd Ferdows Cultural and Artistic Festival , receiving an official Certificate of Appreciationz from the Education Department of District 4 of Tehran.

Since then, I’ve continued developing my skills in Unity , Blender , 3D modeling , character design , and game development .

I enjoy building distinctive characters and dark, atmospheric worlds, while exploring both the creative and technical sides of game creation.



RAEVOR is my personal creative space and the foundation for my future projects. My long-term goal is to become an independent game developer and create, develop, and publish my own games.
